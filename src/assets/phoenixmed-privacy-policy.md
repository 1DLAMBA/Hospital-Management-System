# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026
**Last Updated:** April 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Phoenix ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform at phoenixmed.online.

Please read this policy carefully. By using Phoenix, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Personal Information
- Full name, email address, phone number
- Date of birth and gender
- Account login credentials

### b. Medical & Health Information
- Medical history, past prescriptions, and doctor notes
- Appointment records and doctor communications
- Symptoms and health queries submitted to our AI Health Assistant

### c. Technical Information
- IP address, browser type, and device information
- Cookies and usage data (pages visited, time spent, actions taken)
- Log data and analytics

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Phoenix platform
- Facilitate appointment bookings between patients and licensed doctors
- Enable real-time doctor-patient communication
- Power the AI Health Assistant
- Send appointment confirmations, reminders, and platform notifications
- Improve and personalize your experience
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal or medical data. We may share your information with:

- **Licensed medical professionals** on our platform, as necessary to provide care
- **Service providers** who assist in operating our platform (e.g., cloud storage, analytics) - bound by confidentiality agreements
- **Legal authorities** if required by law, court order, or to protect user safety
- **Business successors** in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

- Encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS)
- Secure storage of medical records
- Access controls limiting who can view sensitive data

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact us immediately at privacy@phoenixmed.online.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal and medical data for as long as your account is active or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our platform. You can control cookie settings through your browser. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **privacy@phoenixmed.online**

Phoenix is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.
