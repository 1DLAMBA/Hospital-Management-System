Before you start

  • Videos are in docs/ad-creatives/video/. Static images are in docs/ad-creatives/png/.
  • Rows marked Feed export name a canvas artboard — open the design canvas, select that artboard and Export PNG.
  • Every video is silent. Add a track in each app’s own composer before posting — TikTok especially punishes silence.
  • The consult video still shows an invented doctor name. Swap in a real verified professional first.
  • Post to TikTok organically, not as ads. Watch what gets past three seconds, then put budget behind that one.

What this plan is not

  • It is not the paid campaign. Google Search and Meta ads run separately, on their own budget and measurement.
  • It has no clinical claims to review — every fact here is scope of practice or a statement about how Phoenix works.
